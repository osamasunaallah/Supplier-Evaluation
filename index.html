i.
The story

Choosing suppliers on facts, not feelings.

Every week the company places orders with dozens of suppliers, some through formal purchase orders and some paid in cash on the spot. The data existed, but it was spread over hundreds of lines in different sheets, with the same supplier often spelled three different ways.

Simple questions had no quick answer: how much have we bought from this supplier, how much of it has actually arrived, and how many days do they really take to deliver?

I built the Supplier Evaluation sheet to answer those questions instantly. Pick a supplier and the sheet gathers every line ever ordered from them, applies VAT, splits spend by currency, and measures lead time from the PO date to the day the goods were received.

It runs entirely on modern Excel formulas, so it updates the moment a new order or delivery is recorded. Nobody has to remember to refresh it.

The method

From scattered lines to one verdict.

Four steps, all inside one sheet and all driven by formulas.

I

Collect every purchase

PO lines from the PO Tracker are stacked with cash purchases from open and closed purchase requests, so nothing bought is missed.

Clean and normalise

A supplier master maps old names and aliases to one official name. Job numbers and dates are filled down through each order block.

Value and time each line

VAT is applied to local purchases, line values are calculated, and delivery days are measured from PO date to received date.

Score the chosen supplier

One dropdown filters everything into five KPIs and a complete item history, recalculated instantly.

    iv.
    Under the hood

    Modern Excel, no macros.

    Every number on the scorecard comes from dynamic array formulas, recalculated the moment a PO is added or received.

    // I · Spend split by currency → "SAR 48,210 + USD 1,150"
=LET(m,   Supplier = $C$8,
     cur, UNIQUE(FILTER(Currency, m)),
     TEXTJOIN(" + ", TRUE,
       MAP(cur, LAMBDA(c,
         c & " " & TEXT(SUM(FILTER(Value,
                m * (Currency = c))), "#,##0")))))

// II · Average delivery days
=LET(d, IF(ISNUMBER(RecvDate), RecvDate - PODate, ""),
     ROUND(AVERAGE(FILTER(d,
       (Supplier = $C$8) * ISNUMBER(d))), 0))

// III · Item history
=FILTER(HSTACK(PO, Job, Item, Qty, Unit,
          Price * (1 + VAT_Rate), Value,
          PODate, RecvDate, Days),
        Supplier = $C$8, "")
    1. Multi-currency spendLocal purchases include VAT. Foreign currencies are totalled separately and never mixed.
    2. Real lead timesMeasured only on received lines, from PO date (or PR date for cash buys) to receipt.
    3. Full historyEvery line from every source, filtered to one supplier in a single spill range.
